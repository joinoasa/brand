# OASA Core Values

**Welcome to the era of endless frontiers. Now build it.**

OASA builds open autonomous systems that make American communities, and the nation as a whole, better, cleaner and safer, and that carry humanity farther than it has ever gone.

---

## Three pillars

**01 · Resilience**
Protect lives. Strengthen American infrastructure. Take on environmental challenges. Be ready for the unexpected.

**02 · Ingenuity**
Advance American engineering and domestic manufacturing. Build on open standards. Teach the skills. Invent together.

**03 · Exploration**
Push autonomous systems across land, air, sea and space, to places no one has reached before.

## Two missions

**Protect and advance life on Earth.** Help communities survive disasters, guard critical infrastructure, explore our oceans and solve the problems that threaten our future.

**Build our future among the stars.** Create the robots, networks and exploration systems that take humanity beyond Earth, one bolder mission at a time.

---

## How we build

- **People first.** Safety comes before speed, autonomy and everything else. When a machine isn't sure, it stops and asks.
- **Secure, always.** Every link is encrypted. No single server, company or person can take the system down. We try to break our own work before anyone else does.
- **Open by default.** Designs, code and docs are published. We build on great open work before reinventing it, and anyone can reproduce what we make.
- **Built to last.** Repairable, documented, affordable, made in America, and ready to build at scale by any chapter.
- **One picture.** Every system shows up on the same shared map, and anyone can fly or drive what runs on its own.

## How we work together

- **Builders first.** Doing beats talking.
- **Dream big, ship small.** Huge visions, built one step at a time.
- **Lift others.** Teach, mentor, share credit.
- **Open and honest.** Share failures as freely as wins.
- **Mission over ego.** The work matters more than who gets the credit.
