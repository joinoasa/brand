# Using the OASA name and logos

The art in this repo is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE). The **OASA name, wordmark, marks and seal** are different: they identify the Open Autonomous Systems Alliance, so they aren't covered by that license.

**Fine without asking**
- Linking to or writing about OASA, and showing the logo next to it.
- Members and chapters using the logo for OASA projects and events.
- Mockups and concept art for this repo.

**Ask first** (email hello@joinoasa.org)
- Merchandise, or anything you sell.
- Anything that suggests OASA made, endorses or certifies your product, project or group.

**Don't**
- Change the logo and present it as official.
- Put the OASA name or logos on anything with weapons.
