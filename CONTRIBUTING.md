# Contributing art

Anyone can suggest or add brand ideas. You don't need to be a member.

## Two ways to help

- **Share an idea or a rough sketch.** Open an [Art idea](https://github.com/joinoasa/brand/issues/new?template=art-idea.yml) issue. A photo of a napkin sketch is fine.
- **Add finished files.** Open a pull request that adds them to the right folder (see the table in the [README](README.md)).

## File rules

- **Logos and icons:** SVG please, plus a PNG preview. A PNG alone is fine for a first idea.
- **Art, patches and banners:** PNG or JPG, at least 1200 px on the long side, under 10 MB.
- **Names:** lowercase with dashes, e.g. `air-patch-v2.png`.
- **Source files** (Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape, Krita): welcome. Put them next to the export.
- New work starts as a **concept**. The core team marks something **Official** in the README once it's chosen.

## Content rules

- **No weapons.** Rugged and field-ready is great. Guns, missiles, weapon mounts, targeting and military insignia are not.
- **Only your own work.** No other brands, logos, characters or copyrighted art. If you used AI tools or someone else's open-licensed work, say so in the pull request.
- Follow the [code of conduct](https://joinoasa.org/docs/code-of-conduct/).

## Pull requests

- One idea per pull request.
- Say what it's for (website, Discord, a project, print) and add a preview image.
- By contributing, you agree your art is shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE).
